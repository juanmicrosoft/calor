Parent: #1370. Depends on M0-R0.

## Purpose

A formal M0 classification requires a consenting independent human methods reviewer and rules frozen before decision-bearing evidence is inspected.

## Work

- Obtain reviewer consent within the R0 authority, or record that the bounded search ended without one.
- Record competence, independence, conflicts, scope, hours, deadline, withdrawal terms, and countersignature format.
- Freeze a versioned gate table covering cost, completion, safety, trust, usable adoption, and independent handoff.
- Freeze alpha/error control, positive-branch logic, missingness and invalid-epoch rules, amendment control, and the four registered classifications.
- Define the redacted public record and restricted evidence manifest.

## Closure paths

- `MET`: an eligible reviewer consents and countersigns the frozen rules.
- `UNAVAILABLE`: the authorized search completes without an eligible consenting reviewer.
- `EXPIRED`: consent or review authority expires.

Closing this issue never fabricates sign-off and never authorizes implementation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Reviewer identity is handled according to R0 privacy rules; the public record contains sufficient role and independence evidence.
- [ ] Conflicts and recusal rules are recorded.
- [ ] Rules are versioned and frozen before R2B inventory inspection or R4 sizing.
- [ ] Amendments require a new version and explicit disposition of affected evidence.
- [ ] Gate value and countersignature/unavailability evidence are linked from #1370.
