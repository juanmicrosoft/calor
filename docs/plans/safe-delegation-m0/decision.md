## Arithmetic interpretation

The roadmap's 368/183/72/36 figures are zero-event single-rate illustrations
for 1/2/5/10 percentage-point margins. The paired completion and log-cost
figures are also scenarios. They are not a completed paired, clustered,
multi-gate power analysis and do not establish a universal lower bound.

Within the displayed illustration, even 80 independent zero-event observations
have a one-sided 97.5% bound of 4.51%, so that scenario does not meet the
original 1-point safety gate. That is enough to reject the illustrated capacity
as a justified implementation route. It is not proof that no alternative
valid paired/clustered design could exist.

No authorized defensible scenario can be sized because the following required
inputs are absent:

- actual historical and future source-task supply;
- pilot and final reservations;
- review duration and feasible reviewer assignment;
- event rates, discordance, cost variation, and source/reviewer dependence;
- setup bands, role rates, and accounting basis;
- executable estimators, undefined-ratio rules, and joint simulation;
- adopter, independent oracle owner, methods reviewer, and decision deadline.

Any later sizing must cover both positive branches; C/B and C/A comparisons
for the original Calor claim; B/A for the workflow-only branch; every cost,
completion, safety, trust, usable-adoption, and independent-handoff component;
accepted and all-assigned safety denominators; paired reviews and
source/reviewer clustering; and the least-favorable approved setup/rate
combination.
