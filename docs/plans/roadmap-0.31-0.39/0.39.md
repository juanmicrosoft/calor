Deliver the next capability justified by external pilot demand and measured outcomes, not architecture preference. A failed or inconclusive experiment may narrow, redirect, or stop a proposed investment.

This milestone is entered only when both entry criteria below are met. Until then it is held: its issues stay open in the 0.39 milestone and only what "While held" allows may be done. The order of milestones, the fallback for the case that no external maintainer consents, and the cases "0.37 verdict not collected" and "0.37 verdict not positive" are written in advance in the plan of record, `docs/plans/roadmap-0.31-0.39.md` (#1765); this epic cites that document and does not restate its fallback table.

**Entry criteria.** Both are read from published records and are not re-judged here.

1. **The 0.37 verdict is positive.** Both must hold:
   - #1712 is closed and its language investment verdict comes from a comparison that was collected: the collection budget decision (#1867) was authorised, and the run was the full 0.37 comparison, not the fallback scope.
   - The verdict names at least one task class in which Calor added value beyond semantically equipped C#, that is, C# given comparable explicit specifications and validation, the single gating comparator of 0.37.

   #1712 also records a proceed, narrow, revise or stop decision for 0.38. That decision governs the entry of #1699 (0.38) and is not read here. This milestone may therefore be entered while 0.38 is in progress, or while 0.38 is held because that decision was `revise` or `stop` or because the task class with demonstrated value is not one 0.38 could search.
2. **The pilot is continuing.** The component accepted under #1697 (0.36) is still maintained as Calor source by a named maintainer outside the project: its `.calr` files are the maintained source, the generated C# is build output, and the component has not been permanently exported to maintained C# (the export #1707 documents; a temporary emergency fork under #1707's rules is not an export). The external maintainer says so in a dated statement that is no older than the #1712 verdict. Maintenance by Calor's creator does not meet this criterion (#1701).

When both hold, record it in one comment on this epic that links the verdict, names the task class and links the external maintainer's statement. That comment releases #1718. It selects no investment and authorises no spend.

The pilot is an entry criterion because every child needs it: #1718 is accepted only when a named external maintainer confirms the need, #1719 opens only on a named external pilot's blocking requirement, and #1720 is accepted only when the named maintainer uses and accepts the capability in an externally maintained component.

**Hold rule.** In each of the following cases the milestone stays held, and the case is recorded in a comment on this epic with a link to the record that establishes it:

- **(a) The adopter fallback applies.** The adopter checkpoint at the close of 0.33 (#1833) recorded "demand unproven" instead of the consent of a named external maintainer, or the outreach decision (#1777) was declined, or left at its default line "not decided, treated as decline until changed", and the plan of record holds this milestone from that date. The fallback table in the plan of record holds 0.39. Nothing published under the fallback's 0.37 scope meets criterion 1, and with no pilot criterion 2 cannot be met.
- **(b) The verdict was not collected.** The collection budget decision (#1867) recorded a decline, in any of the forms that issue defines, so #1712 did not run and #1698 records the verdict as not collected.
- **(c) The verdict is not positive.** #1712 published a verdict that names no task class in which Calor added value beyond equipped C#: it is negative or unresolved for every task class. Its decision for 0.38 does not settle this case: a `revise` or `stop` for 0.38 beside a task class with demonstrated value still meets criterion 1.
- **(d) The pilot is not continuing.** The external maintainer has withdrawn, the component has been permanently exported to maintained C#, or the statement criterion 2 asks for has not been given.

**While held.**
- #1719 and #1720 do not start. No implementation, native prototype, participant work or paid work happens under this epic.
- #1718 stays open with no decision recorded. Its body allows evidence gathering and user interviews before its final decision. Under the hold, drafting from results that are already published is text only and may proceed. An interview is contact with a person outside the project: it takes place only with the pilot's external maintainer while the pilot is continuing, or with a contact the outreach decision (#1777) authorised.
- A hold is not the stop, narrow or redirect that #1718 may record. That decision is made inside an entered milestone, from a positive verdict and observed pilot demand. A held milestone is reported as held, with its case and record, and never as an investment decision or a delivery.
- Work outside this epic is unaffected: existing correctness and stabilization work continues (Non-goals).

**Lifting or ending the hold.** The hold lifts only when both entry criteria are recorded. In case (a) that first requires the 0.36 hold to lift, which needs a later dated entry by Calor's maintainer on the #1833 record that names a consenting external maintainer (see #1697), and then 0.36 and the full 0.37 comparison to complete. In case (b) it requires the collection budget to be authorised later and #1712 to run. In case (c) it requires a further comparison under the 0.37 protocol (#1708), with its own budget decision, to publish a positive verdict; the plan of record schedules none, so it would be filed as new work. In case (d) it requires a named maintainer outside the project to resume or take over maintenance of the component, with consent and source ownership recorded as #1701 requires. The hold has no timer. If Calor's maintainer decides this epic will not be pursued (a negative verdict from #1712, or the end of the pilot, is the occasion to decide), close the epic and its open children as not planned and link the record that caused the hold.

**Exit.** Once entered, the milestone exits on exactly one of three records. The record is posted as a comment on this epic and entered in the evidence ledger, the repository's list of published and adjudicated results (#1774):

1. **Delivered.** #1720 is closed as completed: the named maintainer used and accepted the selected capability against the criteria recorded before implementation, and the results, costs and the expand/narrow/redirect/stop recommendation are published in the release notes of the 0.39 release. The Acceptance items below are met.

3. **Stopped before delivery.** #1718 selected an investment and the investment was stopped before useful delivery; #1720 is closed as not planned and the outcome is reported in the release notes of the 0.39 release (the fourth Acceptance bullet of #1720).
