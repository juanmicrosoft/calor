## Epic #1699 · [0.38] Epic: earn greater autonomy

Existing epic #1699. The body below replaces or amends it as marked.

#### Outcome
Test whether bounded implementation search and evidence-assisted review produce additional accepted improvements economically, and whether accepted change scope can grow without proportional human supervision or weakened acceptance quality.

#### Entry criteria and hold rule
This milestone is entered only when the entry criterion below is met. Until then its issues stay open in the 0.38 milestone and only what "While held" allows may be done. The order of milestones, the adopter fallback, and the cases "0.37 verdict not collected" and "0.37 verdict not positive" are written in advance in the plan of record, `docs/plans/roadmap-0.31-0.39.md` (#1765); this epic cites that document and does not restate its fallback table.

**Entry criterion.** #1712 (0.37) has published its language investment verdict, and the verdict is positive for the task class this epic would search. "Positive" is read from the published records and is not re-judged here. All three must hold:

1. #1712 is closed as completed and #1698 carries its "verdict published" exit record. The verdict comes from the full 0.37 comparison, collected under an authorised budget decision (#1867), not from the fallback scope.
2. The decision #1712 records for 0.38 is `proceed` or `narrow`. (#1712 records one of proceed, narrow, revise or stop, with an owner and a rationale.)
3. The verdict identifies a supported task class: at least one task class in which Calor added value beyond semantically equipped C#, under the readings written before collection (the #1708 protocol, registered with #1867). Semantically equipped C# is C# given comparable explicit specifications and validation; it is the single gating comparator of 0.37 (#1698). #1713 may select only that task class or a subset of it.

When all three hold, record it in a comment on this epic that links the verdict and names the task class. That comment releases #1713. It authorises no spend (see Authorization).

**Hold rule.** While 0.37 is still in progress the milestone is not yet entered and no record is needed. In each of the following cases it is held, and the case is recorded in a comment on this epic with a link to the record that establishes it:

- **(a) The adopter fallback applies.** The adopter checkpoint at the close of 0.33 (#1833) recorded "demand unproven" instead of the consent of a named external maintainer, or the outreach decision (#1777) was declined and the fallback was applied early. The fallback table in the plan of record holds 0.38. Nothing published under the fallback's 0.37 scope meets the entry criterion: item 1 requires the full comparison, and without a pilot component the source ownership (#1701) and recovery path (#1707) that this epic must preserve do not exist.
- **(b) The verdict was not collected.** The collection budget decision (#1867) recorded a decline, so #1712 was closed as not planned and #1698 records the verdict as "not collected".
- **(c) The verdict is not positive.** #1712 recorded `revise` or `stop`, or its verdict is negative or unresolved for every task class #1713 could select.

**While held.**
- #1714, #1715, #1716 and #1717 do not start. No implementation, participant work or paid collection happens under this epic.
- #1713 stays open and unapproved. Protocol drafting is text only. In case (c) #1713 may record a revised hypothesis, or a stop/redirect, which ends the epic as "Lifting or ending the hold" describes.
- Approval of a revised hypothesis starts no implementation and no collection, and does not meet the entry criterion (#1713). #1717 accepts only a `proceed` or `narrow` decision.

**Lifting or ending the hold.** The hold lifts only when the entry criterion is met. In case (a) that first requires the 0.36 hold to lift, through a later dated maintainer entry on the #1833 record that names a consenting external maintainer (see #1697), and then 0.36 and the full 0.37 comparison to complete. In case (b) it requires a later dated authorise entry on the #1867 record, and #1712 to run and publish. In case (c) it requires a further no-search comparison under the #1698 protocol, with its own budget decision, to publish a positive verdict; no issue in milestones 0.31 to 0.39 schedules one, so it would be filed as new work. The hold has no timer, and a held milestone is not reported as delivered. If the maintainer records that this epic will not be pursued (a `stop` from #1712, or a stop/redirect recorded in #1713 under the hold, is the occasion to decide), close the epic and its open children as not planned and link the record that caused the hold.

#### Work breakdown
Child issues cover task selection, a six-condition protocol and the maintainer's decision on its cost limits and spend; bounded search and cost accounting; independent candidate validation and artifact evidence; staged review with blinded defect assessment; and approved execution plus an autonomy decision.

#### Comparison
Extend the 0.37 three authoring conditions with and without search. Calor-plus-search must be compared with C# given equivalent search and validation, not only Calor without search. Reuse 0.37 assets where still valid; record any changed model, task, or tooling conditions rather than treating historical runs as concurrent controls.

#### Acceptance
- Humans retain authorization over public obligations, independent tests, and budgets; agents may propose implementation candidates and separate obligation changes.
- Bound candidate count, elapsed time, inference spend, build/solver effort, and benchmarking. Include review, rework, and recovery costs.
- Evaluate held-out performance and behavior, separate incorrect-specification detection from incorrect-implementation detection, and retain explicit unknown/unchecked boundaries.
- Progress from conventional review to evidence-assisted review; evidence-led review is justified only for bounded cases. Never forbid body inspection or reward zero reads.
- Publish gains, costs, failures, uncertainty, and the decision about whether greater autonomy is warranted.

#### Exit record
Once entered, the milestone exits on exactly one of three records, posted as a comment on this epic and entered in the evidence ledger (#1774):

1. **Decision published.** #1717 is closed as completed, with its results and its owned expand/narrow/revise/stop decision published in the 0.38 release notes and entered in the evidence ledger (#1774), and the Acceptance items above are met.
2. **Stop or redirect.** #1713 is closed with a recorded stop/redirect because no justified task or hypothesis remained (its last Acceptance bullet), and #1714 to #1717 are closed as not planned with that record linked.
3. **Budget declined.** The collection authorisation in the #1713 decision record is a decline: an explicit decline, or the dated default "not decided, treated as decline until changed". #1717 and whichever of #1714 to #1716 are still open are closed as not planned with that record linked, merged work is kept, and this epic records the comparison as "not collected".

Records 2 and 3 are honest conclusions, not a delivered capability. A milestone that was never entered has no exit record: it stays held, or ends as "Lifting or ending the hold" describes. #1718 (0.39) reads whichever of these records exists when it makes its decision. 0.39 is not gated on this milestone: its entry needs a positive 0.37 verdict and a continuing pilot (#1700), so it does not wait for one of these records.

A negative, unresolved or uncollected 0.37 result holds this milestone under the rule above; a revised hypothesis may be written under the hold and is not progression.
