## S2 — the conversion denominator: raw binder bag vs the production rule

**Two rules, stated first.** `Calor0425CorpusLedgerTests.cs:347-354` gates on the *raw* binder bag
(`new Binder(bag).Bind(module); if (bag.HasErrors) skip`). The shipping compiler does not: it
applies `BindingDiagnosticPolicy.PropagateCompilationErrors` (`Program.cs:820`; allowlist
`Binding/Scope.cs:53-78` — duplicate definition / duplicate signature / ambiguous / no matching
overload (Calor0208) / bind-requires-type (Calor0250) / static context / Calor0404 / Calor0405)
*before* the stop at `Program.cs:829-833`. Calor0200, Calor0273, Calor0272 and the #1097 ICE
(Calor0932) are **never propagated**, and a C# `null` emitted as the identifier `null` is the
language's own convention (`RoslynSyntaxVisitor.cs:8850`, `Parser.cs:2691`, `CSharpEmitter.cs:3403`)
— none of them is a user-visible failure. The round-2 engineering review found this; both rules
were then re-measured for this draft.

**S2.1 — in-process, the ledger pipeline verbatim** (`:251-354`; Lossy /
`SelectActiveBranchLossy` / empty symbols; 364 files; 11 s), with `PropagateCompilationErrors`
into a fresh bag beside the raw bag:

| Subject | Files | Parse-failed | Raw-bag bind-failed | **Propagated bind-failed** (first code) | **Reach the effect pass (propagated)** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MediatR | 36 | 3 | 7 | **4** (Calor0208 4) | **29** |
| serilog | 112 | 8 | 57 | **20** (Calor0208 14, Calor0250 5, Calor0201 1) | **84** |
| FluentValidation | 216 | 48 | 142 | **25** (Calor0208 14, Calor0201 7, Calor0250 4) | **143** |
| **Total** | **364** | **59** (Calor0099 36 / 0100 21 / 0117 2) | **206** | **49** | **256** of 305 parsed |
