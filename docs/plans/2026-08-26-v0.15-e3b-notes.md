# v0.15 E3 slice b — rank-1 instantiation (site 6), ρ_body, and the last two Calor0419s

Working notes for `feat/v0.15-e3b-rank1-body-row`. Branched from `9119397e`
(E3 slice a, PR #1103). Governing document:
`docs/design/effect-rows-in-the-type-system.md` — §7 in full, §5, §6.2's
sites 4/5 residual, §8.2, §13.2 (P14, P15, P18, P23), §13.5(a).

## What this slice is

1. **Site 6 — rank-1 effect instantiation.** At a call to a declaration that
   carries `eff` binders, each variable is solved ONCE from the argument rows
   and the instantiated own-row is charged to the caller.
2. **ρ_body (P14).** The effect pass computes each `§LAM`'s body row and checks
   it against the declared `§E` with Calor0410; an un-annotated lambda's type
   row becomes ρ_body instead of E2b/E3a's `Unknown` placeholder.
3. **The external-base Calor0419s retire together** (§6.2, sites 4 and 5) in
   favour of Calor0425.
4. **`RowSiteChecker.IsFunctionTyped` consults the bound `FunctionBoundType`
   first** (§8.2), so `VariableSymbol.FunctionType` and
   `FunctionSymbol.ReturnFunctionType` gain their first production readers.

## Decisions taken here, and why

### D1 — the ordinal is the identity of an effect variable

E2 slice b kept only the NAME of a variable mentioned in a row
(`EffectsNode.EffectVariables`), which is why slice a could only *decline* to
compare an interface's `eff e` with an implementation's `eff f`. Slice b
persists the binder POSITION alongside it
(`EffectsNode.EffectVariableOrdinals`, resolved by `Parser.ResolveEffectVariable`).

The ordinal is the index within the declaration's **`eff` list alone**, not
within the combined type-parameter list that `EffectParameterInfo.Ordinal`
records — that one is the emitter's interleaving position, and reusing it would
make `<T, eff e>` and `<eff e>` stop unifying for a reason that has nothing to do
with effects.
