## What a HIT means

A pass on leg B means **"no large tax detected"**, never "proven equal" — A-1.11's own words. At
this design (4 clusters × 5 runs/arm; realized CV 0.27 here against the 0.44 the margin was
calibrated on) the measured power is **0.22 / 0.48 / 0.77** at true 1.25× / 1.4× / 1.6×
regressions, so a true 25 % tax would slip through more often than not. What the numbers do say:
the point estimate of **1.18** is a real, visible cost on three of the four pairs, and **N1-002's
1.51 is a real per-pair cost** — inside the frozen margin, with a bound of 0.90 that does not
clear 1.0, but not noise to be explained away. And the N1 fixtures carry zero contracts and no
higher-order code: leg B measures a stricter compiler's cost in the edit-compile loop on ordinary
code, **not** rows' cost on rows-using code, which no archived epoch measures. The UNDERPOWERED
branch exists so that a point-only exceedance is never adjudicated as a clean miss; this run did
not need it.
