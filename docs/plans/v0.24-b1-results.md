observed behavior, or build). It does not say which arm is wrong, and part of it reflects the
oracle's strict v1 rules (exact member keys and parameter types, exact exception types).

`pair-manifest.json` carries every registered pair field unchanged plus `registeredDisposition`,

surface, up to 5 witnesses), and `reviewer`. It names the registration's `pairManifestSha256`
(`0f0d760a…`) and the SHA-256 of both oracle outputs. `sha256.json` seals every results file.

## Comparative metric (registered; computed over EQUIVALENT pairs only)

TokenEconomics `CompositeTokenEconomics`: `r = csharpSize / calorSize`, one size rule for both
files. `r > 1` means the C# file is larger. Population: the 17 EQUIVALENT `manifest.benchmarks`
pairs. Sampling unit: the program pair.

| Scope | Pairs | Geometric mean r | Corpus-resampling interval (95%) | Median r | r > 1 / r < 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All included | 17 | 1.235 | [1.115, 1.357] | 1.322 | 13 / 4 |
| TokenEconomics | 15 | 1.265 | [1.148, 1.387] | 1.322 | 12 / 3 |
| ErrorHandling | 1 | 1.343 | none (fewer than 2 pairs) | 1.343 | 1 / 0 |
| PatternMatching | 1 | 0.795 | none (fewer than 2 pairs) | 0.795 | 0 / 1 |

The interval is the registered percentile bootstrap over pairs (SplitMix64 seed 1276, 10,000
resamples, ranks 250 and 9,750). It describes resampling sensitivity of this fixed corpus only and
is not a confidence interval for any population. Exact values are in `results.json`. The validator
recomputes them independently from the committed metric runs.

The 7 descriptive-only metrics are not reported. `metric-set.json` makes reporting them optional,
and this result reports none.

## What is not claimed

- **No correctness claim.** EQUIVALENT means the two arms showed no observable difference on the
  registered finite input set. It is not a proof of equivalence on all inputs, and it says nothing
  about whether either arm implements its task statement (contract §5, amendment 1.1.0).
- **Corpus only.** The corpus is fixed and author-built. It is not a sample of real-world code,
  tasks, or developers, and no figure here generalizes beyond these files.
- **The included set is not the corpus.** The 17 included pairs are the ones the v1 oracle could
  drive and found agreeing: small static-method programs, 15 of them from TokenEconomics. The metric
  describes those 17 pairs, not the 217 pairs sent to the oracle.
- **Not an outcome or an advantage.** `r` is a static size ratio of two committed files. It is not a
  coding-agent outcome and not a measured language, productivity, correctness, or safety advantage.
- **Not comparable with earlier numbers.** No earlier benchmark result shares this comparability key
  (`results.json` `comparability`). Historical benchmark artifacts
  (`website/public/data/benchmark-results.json` and derivatives) are untouched and stay historical.

- R005: the comparability key; metric-run provenance and bit-identity; every per-pair value

  registered population, sampling-unit, and interval wording.

Each rule has one-fact negative controls that assert the rule's code and subject.
