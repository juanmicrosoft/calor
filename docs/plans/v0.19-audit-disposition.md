## Retained limits and research consequences

- Unsupported migration compositions may be counted C# preservation or explicit
  rejection, not native Calor support. #1252 records separate acceptance of
  20 boundaries/180 source expressions and the later 11/268 integration delta.
- The conditional-access D-S1.5 runtime fixture disables effect enforcement;
  its runtime result is not effect verification or default-CLI success.
- #1241's inherited optional Tier 2 corpus-workflow defects remain open.
  They were not silently declared fixed by the release.
- #1276 records non-equivalent legacy benchmark source pairs and invalid
  uncertainty interpretations. The static composite is not agent-value evidence.
- No whole-language soundness, complete cross-file effect checking, or production
  adoption approval follows from these closures.

For any later protected-workflow study, the owner must map **each admitted
construct/property** to discriminating pre-fix and pinned-compiler regressions,
and enforce exclusions where the guarantee is not supported. No child finding
is silently transferred to the research epic. Standalone #1311 owns the newly
proposed bounded false-established search independently of adopter recruitment.
