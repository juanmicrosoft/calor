Milestone 0.20 reviewed whether Calor had enough evidence and authority for a
three-arm adopter study: ordinary C#, strong protected C#, and protected Calor.
It was a planning and evidence tranche, not a compiler release.

## Recorded Outcome

The formal M0 result is **UNADJUDICATED**. The maintainer recorded an
**administrative stop** because the prerequisites for a scientific decision
were absent:

- no consenting independent human methods reviewer;
- no committed adopter or approved recruitment;
- no accepted scientific resource cap or inquiry deadline;
- no provenance-bearing source-task inventory or reviewer schedule;
- no completed joint sizing for the cost, completion, safety, and adoption
  gates.

Missing inputs were not converted into **NOT FEASIBLE**, zero demand, or a
successful implementation.

## What M0 Did Establish

- v0.18.0 and v0.19.0 predecessor outcomes and retained limits were
  reconciled.
- Plausible cost mechanisms for protected Calor versus protected C#, and
  protected C# versus ordinary C#, were stated in falsifiable terms.
- The legacy held-out harness was found to be a useful implementation
  reference, but not an isolated or independently authorized acceptance
  service.
- Recruitment after the 2026-08-04 Call W decision and eligible historical
  task supply remain unknown.
- At the M0 closeout, the redesigned PP-W-rows proposal was deferred pending
  its own buildability result, bounded spend, null-result acceptance, and approval.

M0 did not establish an economic advantage, safety advantage, adopter handoff,
or whole-compiler soundness.

**Local buildability passed on 2026-09-10.** The subsequent
[reviewed gate closure](https://github.com/juanmicrosoft/calor/pull/1348)
used the frozen v0.18 release criteria. This deterministic engineering check
is not an agent observation. Later task preparation, registration, and
instrument work do not establish a study result.

**Separate financial approval, 2026-09-10: USD250 total, pilot only.** The
[recorded user decision](https://github.com/juanmicrosoft/calor/issues/1259#issuecomment-5618103570)
and [authorization receipt](https://github.com/juanmicrosoft/calor/pull/1387)
accept the registered stopping rules and publication of negative or null
results. The quote left the stage choice unselected, so the recorded
interpretation uses the narrower pilot-only scope. This is not automatic
stage-2 funding, a higher ceiling, or permission to weaken the protocol.

**Operational collection is not admitted.** Trustworthy
[spending enforcement](https://github.com/juanmicrosoft/calor/issues/1378),
compatible reviewed pins, and a defensible full-pilot plan within the cap
remain execution prerequisites. The current assessment is **BUDGET_NOT_RUN**,
not not-buildable, a pilot null/negative, or stage-2 **UNDERPOWERED-CARRIED**.
No redesigned agent collection or benefit result exists. The historical M0
disposition and its separate human/participant approval boundaries are
unchanged. See [current pilot status](/docs/benchmarking/results/#redesigned-pp-w-rows-deferred).

## Release and Work Boundaries

Completing milestone 0.20 did not itself change `Directory.Build.props`, create
a tag, publish a NuGet package, deploy the website, or consume a software
version. At the M0 closeout, the current compiler remained v0.19.0. A later
ordinary software release may include these documentation changes, but that
release is a separate decision with normal build, review, packaging, and
publication gates.

The following boundaries remain:

- #1284-#1309 are closed as not planned. They were not implemented.
- #1311 is independent bounded correctness work.
- M0 did not fund or activate #1254 or #1259. The later pilot-only financial
  decision is separate and does not re-arm M0.
- No `sd-001` epoch, participant recruitment, prototype service, or paid
  collection was created.
